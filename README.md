# NEON DRIFT – The Infinite Run

NEON DRIFT is a 3D endless runner made in Unity. You fly a spaceship through a looping neon sci-fi tunnel, dodge asteroids and collect energy orbs to survive as long as possible.

## How to play

| Key | Action |
|-----|--------|
| W | Move up |
| S | Move down |
| A | Move left |
| D | Move right |

- Dodge the asteroids coming towards you.
- You have **3 lives**. Every asteroid hit costs 1 life.
- Collect **energy orbs** to charge your battery: 3 orbs restore 1 life.
- After a hit, your ship is invincible for 1.5 seconds.
- The longer you survive, the faster the asteroids come.
- Your score is your survival time. The best time is saved as your high score.

## 🎮 Play the game

1. Go to the [latest release](https://github.com/2159863-sys/NeonDrift/releases/latest).
2. Download **NeonDrift_Windows.zip**.
3. Unzip it and double-click **NeonDrift.exe**.

> Windows only. If Windows shows a "Windows protected your PC" warning, click **More info → Run anyway**.

## Features

- Main menu with ship selection (3 ships) and high score
- Looping sci-fi tunnel with cyan and purple neon lighting
- Asteroids that spawn faster over time
- Lives system with energy orb battery
- Game Over screen with restart button
- Background music and sound effects (asteroid crash, energy pickup)
- Best Education branding in the main menu

## Built with

- Unity 6 (6000.3.5f2)
- Universal Render Pipeline (URP)
- Unity Input System
- TextMeshPro

## How to open the project

1. Clone or download this repository.
2. Open the project folder in Unity Hub with Unity 6000.3.5f2.
3. Open the scene `Assets/Scenes/MainMenu.unity`.
4. Press **Play**.
